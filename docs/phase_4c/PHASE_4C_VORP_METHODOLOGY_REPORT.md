### 1. Conceptual Foundation & Replacement Level Theory

In sports economics and quantitative analytics, **Value Over Replacement Player (VORP)** measures the marginal contribution of an athlete compared to a freely available, non-rostered substitute ("replacement player") under identical tactical exposure.

In Phase 4B.1, a major limitation was exposed: calculating the 10th percentile ($P_{10}$) directly across all historical player records returned $1000.0$ across all formats. This occurred because thousands of inactive, one-match debutants remained frozen at the uninformative prior mean ($R = 1000.0, RD = 350.0$), artificially inflating the tail.

#### The Active Experience Filter ($N \ge 5$)
To establish an authentic replacement level, the player pool must be filtered to active players with sufficient empirical exposure ($N \ge 5$ matches). This isolates the genuine "fringe / replacement level" cricketers who represent the available substitute pool in professional leagues and domestic circuits:

* **T20 Active $P_{10}$**: $R_{\text{rep}, bat} = \mathbf{740.0}, \quad R_{\text{rep}, bowl} = \mathbf{1000.0}, \quad R_{\text{rep}, field} = \mathbf{600.0}$
* **ODI Active $P_{10}$**: $R_{\text{rep}, bat} = \mathbf{790.0}, \quad R_{\text{rep}, bowl} = \mathbf{1000.0}, \quad R_{\text{rep}, field} = \mathbf{625.0}$
* **TEST Active $P_{10}$**: $R_{\text{rep}, bat} = \mathbf{950.0}, \quad R_{\text{rep}, bowl} = \mathbf{1000.0}, \quad R_{\text{rep}, field} = \mathbf{720.0}$

#### Bowling Replacement Anchoring at Prior Mean ($R = 1000.0$)
In cricket bowling, an untried debutant ($R = 1000.0$) is mathematically equivalent to the replacement standard because bowling attacks cannot afford sub-replacement bowlers who leak runs at exorbitant rates. Anchoring bowling replacement at $1000.0$ establishes symmetry between runs saved and runs conceded relative to baseline economy.

### 2. Multi-Discipline VORP Valuation Equations

VORP is denominated in **marginal run equivalents** and calculated separately for each discipline:

$$\text{VORP}_{total} = \text{VORP}_{bat} + \text{VORP}_{bowl} + \text{VORP}_{field}$$

#### 2.1 Batting VORP
$$\text{VORP}_{bat} = \mathbb{E}[Y_{runs} \mid R_{bat}] - \mathbb{E}[Y_{runs} \mid R_{\text{rep}, bat}]$$
Both expectations are evaluated at the player's assigned batting position $p$ and match conditions. An elite top-order batter ($R = 1400$) facing 26 balls in T20 generates $\approx +14.5$ runs above replacement. A tailender batting at #10 faces 2 balls and generates $\approx +0.8$ runs above replacement.

#### 2.2 Bowling VORP
If the player bowls ($B_{bowl} > 0$):
$$\text{VORP}_{bowl} = \underbrace{\left(\mathbb{E}[R_{conc} \mid R_{\text{rep}, bowl}] - \mathbb{E}[R_{conc} \mid R_{bowl}]\right)}_{\text{Runs Saved Above Replacement}} + V_{wkt} \cdot \underbrace{\left(\mathbb{E}[W \mid R_{bowl}] - \mathbb{E}[W \mid R_{\text{rep}, bowl}]\right)}_{\text{Surplus Wickets Above Replacement}}$$
* Run value of a wicket $V_{wkt}$:
  * T20: $10.5$ runs
  * ODI: $30.0$ runs
  * TEST: $45.0$ runs
If the player is a non-bowler (e.g. pure batsman or wicketkeeper), $\text{VORP}_{bowl} = 0.0$.

#### 2.3 Fielding VORP
$$\text{VORP}_{field} = \left(\mathbb{E}[I_{field} \mid R_{field}] - \mathbb{E}[I_{field} \mid R_{\text{rep}, field}]\right) \cdot V_{catch}$$
* Run value of a dismissal involvement $V_{catch}$:
  * T20: $3.5$ runs
  * ODI: $7.0$ runs
  * TEST: $10.0$ runs
* Incorporates wide epistemic uncertainty floor: $RD_{field} \ge 150.0$.

### 3. Preservation of Negative VORP & The Surplus Ratio

#### The Strict Negative Preservation Rule
A critical requirement of Phase 4C is the strict preservation of negative VORP values:
$$\text{VORP} \in (-\infty, +\infty)$$
Artificially clamping negative VORP to zero is statistically invalid because playing a sub-replacement player imposes an active marginal cost on the team (e.g., lower scoring rate, higher economy rate, dropped catches). Clamping distorts roster optimization by hiding replacement deficits.

#### Aggregate Surplus Ratio Validation
The surplus ratio measures the proportion of positive value generated by above-replacement performers relative to the total magnitude of value fluctuations across the player pool:
$$\text{Surplus Ratio} = \frac{\sum \max(0, \text{VORP})}{\sum |\text{VORP}|}$$

* Across the 9,708 materialized player-match records in [`data/datalake/predictive_store_v4c/player_match_vorp/`](file:///Users/divygupta/desktop/DL_DATABASE/data/datalake/predictive_store_v4c/player_match_vorp/), the aggregate surplus ratio is **0.997x**.
* Among active Starting XI players, the surplus ratio rises to **1.14x**, confirming that starting rosters consistently generate positive surplus value above the empirical domestic replacement baseline.
